WHAT THE APP IS
R/HOOD is an iPhone DJ community: profiles, gig applications, mixes, connections, and messaging. It is not an iPad app. Please use the iPhone screenshots only.

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1)
Settings → Account → Delete Account. Also available on the waitlist / not-approved screen. This permanently deletes the profile and auth account in-app. No email to support is required.

USER CONTENT (Guideline 1.2)
- Report: Listen → long-press a mix → Report. Another DJ’s profile → ⋯ → Report. Incoming messages → tap the message → Report.
- Block: another DJ’s profile → ⋯ → Block, or long-press a mix → Block. Blocking hides them from Listen, Discover, and Messages immediately and notifies our team.
- We act on reports and blocks within 24 hours (remove content / suspend the offending account). Contact: hello@rhood.io

Please see the attached screen recording: (1) Terms checkbox + Terms of Service before login, (2) Report on a mix or profile, (3) Block on a profile.

CREDITS / BOOSTS (Guideline 3.1.1)
Credits are earned only (referrals, achievements, completed gigs). They cannot be bought. Boosting an application spends earned credits only. There is no Stripe, PayPal, or In-App Purchase for digital goods.

AUDIO
Playback is streamed AAC/MP3. WAV uploads are converted to AAC on the iPhone before they are stored, so mixes should play in-app and on the lock screen. Background audio is the lock-screen / Control Center player.

TRACKING
We do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Mixpanel and Firebase are first-party product analytics only (no IDFA, no session replay for ads).

LOCATION
When In Use only, to match DJs and opportunities in the same city. No Always / background location.
