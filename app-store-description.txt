R/HOOD is the opportunity platform for DJs and producers. Discover gigs, share mixes, connect with other artists, and build a profile through the work you actually do.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Browse and apply for gigs, studio sessions, collaborations, and brand campaigns
• Upload mixes and listen to DJs from the community
• Build a profile with your bio, genres, location, and links
• Message and connect with other DJs
• See opportunities and DJs near you

HOW MEMBERSHIP WORKS

R/HOOD is a community for working DJs and producers. After you create an account, the R/HOOD team reviews your application, and you'll get a notification and an email when you're approved. Have an invite code from R/HOOD or another DJ? Enter it when you sign up to join straight away.

CREDITS

Credits are earned through activity such as referrals, achievements, and completed gigs. They can't be bought with real money, and there are no in-app purchases.
